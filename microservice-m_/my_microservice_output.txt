Ex 19.1 - Architecture -> Kafka: Distributed Commit Log | RabbitMQ: AMQP Message Queue
Ex 19.1 - Persistence & Replay -> Kafka: Retains data on disk (Replayable) | RabbitMQ: Deletes after ACK
Ex 19.2 - RabbitMQ Topic Exchange routed 'order.created' to: [order_queue, audit_queue]

Ex 19.5 - Rule: Choose RabbitMQ for complex AMQP routing & RPC; Choose Kafka for high-throughput event streaming & replay
